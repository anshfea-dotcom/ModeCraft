Invoices & Billing

Issue a permanent invoice against an existing order and download its PDF.

Create an invoice

Choose a real order. The invoice is issued only once per order and its details are preserved.

GST invoice is created only when you press Issue Invoice. Fill in actual GST details. The sum of gross line amounts must equal the order total. Verify tax rates, shipping, discount allocation and any e-invoicing obligations before issue.

No invoice is generated when an order arrives. Only an authorised owner or employee may issue an invoice manually. Each order receives at most one final invoice. Issuing does not alter payment, stock or email customers.
