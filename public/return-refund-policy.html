Return & refund policy.

We want you to feel confident shopping with AERLON. If something is not right with your order, contact us within the request window below.

1. Request window

Return or exchange requests must be submitted within 3 days after the order is delivered. Requests received after this period may not be accepted.

2. How to raise a request

  • Contact AERLON customer care on WhatsApp at +91 96486 29929 within 3 days after delivery.
  • Share your order reference and the mobile number used at checkout.
  • Explain the reason for your request and share clear photos of the item if it is damaged, defective or incorrect.
  • Wait for our team to review the request and guide you on the next steps. Please do not ship an item back before receiving instructions.

3. Review and resolution

Each request is reviewed by our customer-care team. Any return, exchange or refund is subject to confirmation after review. Our team will communicate the applicable next steps and resolution for the specific order.

4. Need assistance?

For all return, exchange and refund requests, contact us through our official customer-care WhatsApp number.
